// Builds apps, tools and websites
 
From Vinje in Telemark. Based in Oslo. Second year of frontend development, with two apps already out: Glimt in open beta on iOS, Paneless for macOS.
 
I believe every project deserves its own identity.
Not a ready-made template with your logo slapped on it.
 
What I care about most:
- That it feels right, not just looks right
- That it's built properly, not just fast
- That someone actually wants to use it
