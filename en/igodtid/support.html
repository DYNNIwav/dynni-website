I god tid: Support

Stuck, or something not working? Email me at support@dynni.no. I'm Pål Omland Eilevstjønn and I built the app myself, so I'm the one who answers.

I can't see my calendars

I god tid needs permission to read your calendars. Open Settings on your iPhone, go to Privacy & Security, then Calendars, and give I god tid full access.

I don't get a travel time

The event needs a place. To work it out from where you are, the app also needs your location: Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services. Public transport times come from Entur and cover Norway.

I bought Pluss, but it isn't unlocked

Open Pluss in the app and tap Restore purchase. The app then asks the App Store what you already own.

I want a refund

Apple handles all purchases, so you ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
